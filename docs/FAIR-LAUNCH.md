# ALIVE Fair Launch Policy

## Commitments

The ALIVE launch is intended to follow these principles:

1. **No creator buy:** the creator will not intentionally purchase $ALIVE.
2. **No creator allocation:** no portion of the token supply is reserved for the creator.
3. **No private presale:** no private token sale or insider allocation is intended.
4. **Public launch:** the token is intended to be launched publicly through Pump.fun.
5. **Disclosure:** creator wallet, mint address, launch transaction, and actual creator-fee configuration will be published when verified.

## Verification checklist

Before launch:
- [ ] Publish the creator wallet address.
- [ ] Confirm the wallet is controlled by the person making this commitment.
- [ ] Record the intended launch settings.
- [ ] Explain any creator-fee or revenue settings accurately.

After launch:
- [ ] Publish the mint address.
- [ ] Publish the launch transaction.
- [ ] Publish the official Pump.fun page.
- [ ] Record actual fee settings and any changes.
- [ ] Review public wallet activity and disclose relevant findings.
- [ ] Correct errors promptly.

## Limits of the commitment

A public wallet review cannot always establish whether undisclosed related wallets or third parties are associated with the creator. Do not describe the launch as “provably fair,” “audited,” or “guaranteed” unless independent evidence supports that exact claim.

Pump.fun's interface, fee model, and token mechanics may change. Confirm the current settings at the time of launch. This repository does not deploy a token or configure Pump.fun on your behalf.
