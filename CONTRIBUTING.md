# Contributing to ALIVE

Thanks for wanting to contribute. ALIVE is an open community project.

## Ways to contribute
- Improve documentation or translations.
- Report inaccurate information or broken links.
- Suggest community activities or creative concepts.
- Build independent dashboards, bots for public information, or integrations.
- Review launch disclosures and submit evidence-based corrections.

## Workflow
1. Search existing issues before opening a new one.
2. For substantial changes, open an issue first to discuss the proposal.
3. Fork the repository and create a focused branch.
4. Keep changes clear, factual, and easy to review.
5. Submit a pull request describing what changed and why.

## Contribution expectations
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Do not submit fake partnership claims, fabricated metrics, or misleading financial claims.
- Do not build or promote wash trading, fake volume, wallet spoofing, or deceptive engagement.
- Do not include private keys, seed phrases, passwords, or personal data.
- A contribution does not grant tokens, compensation, ownership, or governance rights.

By contributing, you agree that your contributions are provided under the repository's MIT license unless stated otherwise.
