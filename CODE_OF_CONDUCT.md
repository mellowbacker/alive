# Community Code of Conduct

We want ALIVE spaces to be open, useful, and respectful.

## Expected behavior
- Treat people with respect.
- Debate ideas, not identities.
- Be honest about uncertainty and disclose conflicts of interest.
- Share verifiable information and correct mistakes.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or intimidation.
- Doxxing, impersonation, or scams.
- Fake endorsements, fabricated partnerships, or deceptive financial claims.
- Spam, manipulation, or coordinated artificial trading activity.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation in project-controlled spaces when necessary. This does not imply control over independent community spaces or token holders.
